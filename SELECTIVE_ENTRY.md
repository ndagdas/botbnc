# Selective observation preset, 15 minutes

Goal: fewer early expansion candidates after a quiet base, rather than chasing a large completed pump. The five supplied screenshots cover four symbols (MOVR appears twice) and are successful examples, not an unbiased backtest. Their right-side RSI/volume panel shows the displayed candle, not necessarily the historical entry. No measured optimal thresholds, win rate or daily signal count is asserted.

New Pine: pine/Pump_Early_Selective_v2_3_4_4.pine. A final gate applies to every existing entry path, including IGNITION. All new parameters remain editable in the selective input group. Defaults: Pump score >=9/11; volume ratio >=1.8; rising RSI 54..72; ADX >=18 and +DI > -DI; rising positive MACD histogram; rising EMA21 and EMA55 with price above them; a recent quiet 12-bar base <=6 previous ATR; a fresh 8-bar breakout <=2 bars old; candle body <=3% and <=1.8 previous ATR, closing in its upper 35%; distance to EMA9 and prior breakout <=1.2 previous ATR; TP1 reward/risk >=1.5; same-symbol Pine cooldown 32 bars (8 hours at 15m). Uses closed bars only; no future bars. Existing exit handling remains unchanged. Export defaults use the user's existing ATR10, SL2.2, TP1=5, TP2=9, TP3=19 preset.

Observer: CANDIDATE_MIN_SCORE=85; MAX_AL_CANDIDATES_PER_DAY=6 across all symbols, counting original AL candidates AND watch-to-AL promotions together. AL_SYMBOL_COOLDOWN_MINUTES=480; CANDIDATE_TIMEZONE=Europe/Istanbul, midnight reset. The cap reserves an accepted candidate, not an executed exchange trade or confirmed Telegram delivery. It selects qualifying candidates as they are processed; it does not claim to identify the best six of the entire future day. A quota- or repeat-blocked AL candidate is logged in its sanitized job result and produces no extra Telegram entry notification. WATCH/status notifications are separate from AL candidates. No minimum daily count is forced.

The reservation and Telegram outbox commit in one database transaction; retries reuse the same reservation. PostgreSQL is REQUIRED to preserve the cap and watches across Heroku restarts. SQLite fallback loses both on dyno replacement; health exposes restart_safe=false. Candidate limits therefore must not be advertised as restart-proof until a permanent database is connected. API and Telegram configuration must also be complete before live alarm activation.

Update/recreate TradingView's alert snapshot after loading the new Pine source, keeping /observer/webhook and the current secret. Do not enter Binance credentials into this observer JSON. Observe in Telegram before enabling any exchange trading. Parameter effectiveness needs raw candle data, losing cases and an out-of-sample cost-inclusive test, not only screenshots.

Validation: TradingView compile plus 22 Python functional tests, including concurrent global cap, idempotent retries, midnight reset, cross-midnight symbol cooldown and Telegram suppression. These are functional tests, not a strategy profitability backtest.
